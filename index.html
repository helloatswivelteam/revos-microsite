FAQ

Questions, answered

Why not just do this with Claude ourselves?

You can — and many teams try. But on your own, AI is an echo chamber: it reflects your assumptions back with confidence, one tactic at a time, without the expertise or judgment to know the right things to do. It doesn’t have your proven plan, our 300+ playbooks, the experts, or eight years of what works. RevOS is the difference between random acts of AI and a proven system that works.

Will I be stuck with you forever?

No. We build and run your RevOS for ~12 months and prove the ROI. After that you move to an operating license and run it yourself — a fraction of the build, and you can stop anytime. You’re not locked in.

Do I own the system, or license it?

You hold an operating license to run the proven system — the plan, the plays that work for your market, and your trained team. We keep the platform live, current, and supported; the underlying IP stays ours, so there’s no reselling. It’s a fraction of the build, and yours to run for as long as it’s driving returns.

What if I don’t have a marketing team?

That’s the common case, and it’s exactly why the Custom Build exists — we build and run the whole system for you. When you’re ready, one or two people can run it.

Why 12 months?

Because we’re not just standing up software — we’re building the strategy, testing channels, segments and messaging, and proving what actually works for your market. At the end you have a method, not a guess.
